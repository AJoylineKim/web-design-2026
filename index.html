The Most Oldest Living Tortoise

Johnathan, Adwaita, Alagaba

Johnathan

Johnathan is currently the verified longest living tortoise. He is 193 years old as of 2026.

Adwaita

Adwaita was believed to be 255 years old. He died in 2006 at the Alipore Zoo in Kolkata, India.

Alagaba

Alagaba was believed to be 344 years old. Many say he lived his full life in the royal palace of Ogbomoso, Nigeria.
